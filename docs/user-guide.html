Chapter 01

Getting Started

This chapter gives you a quick overview of the website — what it does, who uses it, and what you need before you begin.

What is the L-PRES Website?

The Kwara L-PRES website is the official online presence of the Kwara State Livestock Productivity and Resilience Support Project. It serves two purposes:

🌐
Public Website
Anyone on the internet can visit this. It shows news, project updates, the media gallery, programme information, and contact details. Visitors cannot change anything — they can only read.
🔒
Admin Panel (Private)
Only authorised staff with a username and password can access this. This is where you create news articles, upload gallery media, and manage project information.

What You Can Do as an Admin

📰
Publish News
Write and publish news articles, events, and announcements with photos.
🖼
Manage Gallery
Upload photos and videos, organise them by category, and remove outdated media.
📌
Update Projects
Add new field projects, update their status, and upload multiple photos per project.

What You Need to Get Started

1
A modern web browser
Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari. Any of these will work. Make sure your browser is up to date for the best experience.
2
An internet connection
The website is hosted online, so you need internet access both to view it and to manage content through the admin panel.
3
Your admin username and password
You need these to log in to the admin panel. The default credentials are provided in Chapter 10 (Quick Reference Card). Change the password as soon as possible after your first login.

Two Important URLs

💡
Bookmark these URLs
Open the admin panel URL in your browser and save it as a bookmark for quick access. In Chrome, press Ctrl + D (or ⌘ + D on Mac) to bookmark the current page.
Chapter 02

Logging In & Out

The admin panel is protected by a username and password. Your session stays active for 24 hours. After that, you will need to log in again.

How to Log In

1
Open your browser and go to the admin login page
Type this address in your browser's address bar and press Enter:
https://kwara-lpres.netlify.app/admin/login
2
Enter your username
Click on the Username field and type your admin username. The default username is admin.
3
Enter your password
Click on the Password field and type your password. The characters will appear as dots for security — this is normal.
4
Click "Sign In"
Click the green Sign In button. If your credentials are correct, you will be taken to the Admin Dashboard automatically.

Chapter 04

Managing News Articles

The News section is how you communicate with the public — field updates, events, achievements, and partnership news. You can write articles, attach photos, and choose when to publish them.

Creating a New Article

1
Click "News Articles" in the sidebar
This shows a list of all existing articles. Published ones are marked in green. Drafts are marked grey.
2
Click the "+ New Article" button
You will find this button in the top-right corner of the News Articles page. Click it to open the article creation form.
3
Fill in the article details
Complete the fields in the form. See the field guide below for what each field means.
4
Write the article body
The large text area in the middle is the rich text editor. Type your article here. Use the toolbar above it to format text — make headings, add bold/italic, create lists.
5
Upload a featured image (optional but recommended)
Click the "Featured Image" upload button and choose a photo from your computer. This image appears as the article's thumbnail in the news list and at the top of the article page.
6
Choose whether to publish now or save as draft
— Tick "Publish immediately" to make the article live on the website right away.
— Leave it unticked to save as a Draft — only you can see it, visitors cannot.
7
Click "Save Article"
A green confirmation message will appear at the top of the screen when the article is saved successfully.

Article Fields — What to Fill In

Article Form Fields
Title *
The headline of your article. Keep it clear and under 100 characters.
e.g. "L-PRES Teams Complete First Phase of Vaccination Drive"
Article Body *
The full text of your article. Write as much as needed. You can format text using the toolbar.
Use Heading 2 for subheadings, bullet lists for key points.
Category *
Choose the type of content from the dropdown menu.
Options: News, Events, Field Work, Partnership, Training, Publication
Published By *
The author or office publishing this article.
e.g. "State Project Coordinator" or "L-PRES Communications Unit"
Event Date
Only for Events. Enter the date the event happened or will happen.
e.g. "June 20, 2026"
Excerpt
A short summary (1–2 sentences) shown in the news list preview. If you leave this blank, the system will automatically use the first 200 characters of your article body.
Featured Image
The main photo for this article. Accepted: JPG, PNG. Maximum size: 10 MB.
Recommended: landscape photo, at least 800px wide.
Article Template
Choose the layout design for this article page. Template 1 is the default and works for all articles.

Publishing, Unpublishing & Deleting

✅
Publish an Article
In the articles list, click the eye icon (👁) next to a draft article to toggle it from Draft to Published. It will immediately appear on the public website.
🙈
Unpublish an Article
Click the same eye icon again on a published article to hide it from the public. The article is not deleted — it becomes a draft and only you can see it.
🗑
Delete an Article
Click the bin icon (🗑) and confirm when prompted. This cannot be undone. Consider unpublishing instead if you may need the article again.
⚠️
Deletion is permanent
When you delete an article, it is permanently removed from the database and cannot be recovered. If you are unsure, unpublish the article instead — it will be hidden from visitors but remain available for you to edit or re-publish later.

Chapter 05

Managing the Media Gallery

The Gallery is a public-facing collection of photos and videos that showcases L-PRES field activities. Visitors can filter by category and view full-size images or play videos.

Uploading a New Photo or Video

1
Click "Gallery" in the sidebar
You will see a grid of all existing gallery items, with their thumbnails, titles, and category labels.
2
Click "+ Upload Media"
This opens the upload form. You will fill in details and attach your file.
3
Enter the title
Give the photo or video a descriptive title so it can be found easily later.
e.g. "Animal Health Campaign — Baruten LGA, June 2026"
4
Choose the media type
Select Photo for images, or Video for video files. Choosing the wrong type affects how the item is displayed on the gallery page.
5
Choose a category
Select the most appropriate category from the dropdown. This allows visitors to filter the gallery.
Field Work · Outreach · Infrastructure · Events · Training · General
6
Click "Choose File" and select your file
Browse your computer and select the photo or video file. See the limits below for accepted file types and sizes.
7
For videos — also upload a thumbnail image
Videos need a separate thumbnail (a still image shown before the video plays). Click "Choose Thumbnail" and upload a JPG or PNG photo related to the video.
8
Optionally add a description
A brief caption explaining what the photo shows — location, date, people involved. This appears when visitors hover or click on the image.
9
Click "Upload"
The file will upload and appear in the gallery immediately. Large files (especially videos) may take a minute to upload depending on your internet speed.

Accepted File Types & Size Limits

Media TypeAccepted FormatsMaximum File Size
PhotosJPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP15 MB per image
VideosMP4, WEBM, MOV, AVI300 MB per video
ThumbnailsJPG, PNG15 MB
💡
Photo quality tip
Use photos that are landscape orientation (wider than tall) and at least 1000px wide for the best appearance on the website. Avoid blurry or very dark photos — they make the website look unprofessional. Use photos taken in natural daylight where possible.

Editing a Gallery Item

1
Find the item in the gallery list
Scroll through the gallery manager or use the search/filter at the top of the page.
2
Click the pencil icon (✏) next to the item
This opens the edit form pre-filled with the current details.
3
Make your changes and click "Save"
You can update the title, description, category, or replace the file. Changes are immediate.

Hiding and Removing Gallery Items

👁
Hide from Gallery
Click the eye icon to unpublish the item. It is hidden from visitors but remains in the system. You can re-publish it anytime. Good for temporarily removing seasonal content.
🗑
Permanently Delete
Click the bin icon and confirm. The file is permanently deleted from the server. This cannot be undone — only delete items you are certain are no longer needed.
Chapter 06

Managing the Project Portfolio

The Project Portfolio section of the website showcases field projects across Kwara State. Each project can have a cover photo, a gallery of additional photos, highlights, and status information.

Adding a New Project

1
Click "Projects" in the sidebar
This shows a list of all current projects. Each row shows the project name, location, status, and action buttons.
2
Click "+ New Project"
The project creation form will open. Fill in all the required fields.
3
Fill in the project details
Enter the name, LGA, cluster, description, and current status. See the field guide below.
4
Add project highlights
Highlights are short bullet points shown on the project card (e.g., "2,400+ farmers registered"). Type each highlight and press Enter or click "Add" to add it. You can add up to 6 highlights per project.
5
Upload a cover image
This is the main photo shown first on the project card. Click "Choose Cover Image" and select a photo. Recommended: landscape, at least 800px wide.
6
Upload additional gallery photos (optional)
In the "Photo Gallery" section of the form, click "Add Photos" and select multiple images. These appear in the image carousel when visitors browse the project. You can add as many photos as you like.
7
Click "Save Project"
The project is saved and will appear on the public Project Portfolio section of the homepage.

Project Fields — What to Fill In

Project Form Fields
Project Name *
The full name of the project or site.
e.g. "Kwara Cattle Development Centre"
LGA *
The Local Government Area where the project is located.
e.g. "Ilorin East", "Baruten", "Offa"
Cluster
The regional cluster this LGA belongs to.
Options: Kwara Central, Kwara North, Kwara South
Status *
The current implementation status of the project.
Active — ongoing  Planned — not yet started  Completed — finished
Description
A paragraph describing the project — what it does, who it serves, and its impact. Aim for 2–4 sentences.
Highlights
Short bullet-point facts about the project. Each one should be a concrete, specific figure or achievement.
e.g. "2,400+ farmers registered", "24/7 veterinary support", "N250M investment"
Sort Order
A number that controls the order projects appear on the website. Lower numbers appear first (e.g., 1 appears before 2).

Editing Project Gallery Photos

When editing an existing project, you can manage the gallery photos:

❌
Remove an Existing Photo
Existing gallery photos appear as small thumbnails with an × button. Click the × on any photo to mark it for removal. It will only be deleted when you click "Save Project".
➕
Add More Photos
Click "Add Photos" to select new images from your computer. New photos appear with a green "New" badge. They are uploaded when you save the project.
📌
Gallery changes are not saved until you click "Save Project"
Adding or removing photos only takes effect when you click the Save Project button. If you close the form without saving, your photo changes will be lost.

Chapter 08

Content Best Practices

Following these guidelines will keep the website looking professional, consistent, and trustworthy to visitors, partners, and government officials.

Writing Good News Articles

✍️
Write for your readers — not for insiders
Imagine a journalist or World Bank official reading your article for the first time. Avoid acronyms without explanation. Write dates in full (e.g., "June 20, 2026" not "20/6/26"). Mention the location, the number of people involved, and the outcome of every event you write about.
✅
Good Article Title
"L-PRES Vaccinates Over 12,000 Livestock Across Baruten LGA in June Drive"

— Specific number, location, timeframe, and action all in one line.
❌
Weak Article Title
"Programme Activities Update"

— No specific information. Visitors will not click this.

Article Length Guidelines

Article TypeRecommended LengthNotes
News / Field Update200–500 wordsLead with the key fact, then give context
Event Report300–600 wordsInclude date, location, attendees, outcomes
Partnership Story400–700 wordsInclude quotes if available
Training Report200–400 wordsInclude number of participants and topics covered

Photo Guidelines

📐
Use Landscape Photos
Horizontal photos (wider than tall) look best on the website. Portrait photos (taller than wide) may be cropped or appear awkward. Aim for a 16:9 or 3:2 ratio.
☀️
Use Well-Lit Photos
Avoid dark, blurry, or low-resolution images. Photos taken outdoors in natural light are ideal. If indoors, ensure the subject is lit from the front.
📏
Minimum 800px Width
Photos should be at least 800 pixels wide for good quality on desktop screens. Avoid screenshots or photos taken from WhatsApp (these are usually compressed and low-quality).

Naming Your Files

📁
Use descriptive filenames before uploading
Before uploading a photo, rename it to something meaningful on your computer. For example: vaccination-drive-baruten-june2026.jpg instead of IMG_20260615_001.jpg. While the website handles files automatically, good filenames help you stay organised when managing many uploads.

Consistency Checklist

  • ✓Always add a featured image to every news article
  • ✓Use the correct category for every article and gallery item
  • ✓Write a short excerpt (summary) for every article
  • ✓Include the LGA and date in field update articles
  • ✓Update project status when work progresses (Active → Completed)
  • ✓Review articles for spelling before publishing
  • ✓Upload a thumbnail for every video gallery item
  • ✓Unpublish outdated articles rather than deleting them
Chapter 09

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Answers to the most common questions and issues that admins encounter when managing the website.

Login Problems

QI entered my username and password but it says "Incorrect username or password". What do I do?
1. Check that Caps Lock is not on — passwords are case-sensitive.
2. Make sure you are typing the username in lowercase: admin (not Admin or ADMIN).
3. Try copying and pasting the password instead of typing it.
4. If you still cannot log in, contact your IT administrator — the password may need to be reset in the system.
QI was logged in but the system suddenly asked me to log in again. Why?
Admin sessions last 24 hours. After that, the system automatically logs you out for security. Simply log in again with your credentials. Any unsaved work would have been lost — always save your work regularly.

Content Issues

QI published an article but it is not showing on the public website. What happened?
1. Make sure the "Publish immediately" checkbox was ticked when you saved the article.
2. Hard-refresh the public website by pressing Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or ⌘+Shift+R (Mac).
3. In the admin panel, go to News Articles and confirm the article shows a Published badge (not Draft).
QMy uploaded photo is showing but it looks blurry or distorted. How do I fix it?
The original photo may have been low-resolution. Delete the gallery item and re-upload a higher-quality version. Use photos that are at least 800px wide. Avoid screenshots or photos downloaded from WhatsApp — these are heavily compressed.
QI uploaded a photo but nothing happened and no confirmation appeared. Did it upload?
Large files can take time. Wait at least 30 seconds before trying again. Check:
1. Your internet connection is active.
2. The file is not larger than the limit (15 MB for photos, 300 MB for videos).
3. The file type is accepted (JPG, PNG for photos; MP4, MOV for videos).
If the upload keeps failing, try compressing the photo using a tool like squoosh.app before uploading.
QI accidentally deleted an article or gallery item. Can I get it back?
Unfortunately, no. Deleted content cannot be recovered. This is why we recommend unpublishing instead of deleting when you are unsure. For important articles, consider keeping a copy in a Word document on your computer as a backup.
QThe project portfolio on the homepage is not updating when I add a new project. Why?
1. Make sure the new project has "Published" set to Yes (the toggle should be on).
2. Hard-refresh the homepage: Ctrl+Shift+R.
3. Check the project's Sort Order number — if it is very high (e.g., 99), it may appear at the end of the list and not be immediately visible in the slider.

General Website Questions

QThe website looks different on my phone compared to my computer. Is that normal?
Yes, this is completely normal and intentional. The website is responsive — it automatically adjusts its layout depending on the screen size. Some sections stack vertically on mobile instead of side-by-side. The content is the same on all devices.
QCan I change the website's colours, logo, or layout?
The colours, logo, and layout are part of the website's design and require a developer to change. The admin panel only controls content (news articles, gallery media, and project information). For design changes, contact the website development team.
QCan multiple people use the admin panel at the same time?
Yes — multiple admins can be logged in simultaneously. However, if two people edit the same article or project at the same time, the last person to save will overwrite the other's changes. Coordinate with your team to avoid editing the same item simultaneously.
QThe website is not loading at all. What do I do?
1. Check your internet connection — try opening another website like google.com.
2. Try a different browser (e.g., if using Chrome, try Edge or Firefox).
3. Clear your browser cache: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → Clear cached images and files.
4. If the public site is down for everyone, contact the website development team — the hosting provider may be experiencing an outage.
